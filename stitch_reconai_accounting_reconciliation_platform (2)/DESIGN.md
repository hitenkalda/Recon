## Brand & Style
This design system defines an ultra-premium, restrained architectural financial interface engineered for top-tier Chartered Accounting firms, institutional advisory boards, and automated fiscal intelligence engines. The aesthetic fuses unyielding technical minimalism with xAI-grade architectural discipline: pitch-black canvases, razor-thin structural borders, deadpan zero-radius geometry, and surgical typography.

The visual atmosphere rejects decorative clutter, bright neon washes, and rounded consumer-tech tropes in favor of absolute computational gravitas. A pure `#000000` substrate provides infinite contrast, allowing micro-fine graphite delineations and whisper-quiet icy cyan accents (`#67e8f9`) to direct attention with mathematical certainty. The resulting experience evokes institutional luxury, executive authority, and an uncompromising standard of audit precision.

## Colors
The color architecture enforces strict restraint across a deep graphite and true pitch-black value spectrum:

- **Primary (`#67e8f9`)**: An icy, whisper-quiet cyan reserved strictly for active AI reconciliation confirmations, telemetry indicators, active terminal states, and focal audit discrepancies. It must never be applied as an expansive background fill or heavy luminous glow.
- **Secondary (`#ffffff`)**: High-contrast, surgical white dedicated to primary headline typography, confirmed transactional values, and high-priority executive triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#71717a`)**: A muted metallic zinc tone deployed for secondary metadata, column structures, non-active control boundaries, and dimensional borders.
- **Neutral (`#000000`)**: Absolute black canvas baseline, maximizing optical contrast and reducing visual fatigue across lengthy audit sessions.
- **Substrates & Graphite Surfaces**: Layered stepping progresses from true black `#000000` through `#080808` (work panels), `#0d0d0e` (cards and metric containers), up to `#141416` (elevated modals and flyouts).
- **Hairline Borders**: Structural definitions rely solely on micro-fine white alpha channels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` through `0.12`), avoiding opaque gray bands.

## Typography
Typographic pairing divides analytical cognition from narrative framing:

- **Structural Narrative (`Geist`)**: Used across displays, view titles, modal headers, and overarching ledger sections. Tight tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.04em`) produces an architectural, bespoke technical presence.
- **Computational Verification (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Applied to body copy, audit grids, ledger tables, currency notations, GSTIN/PAN strings, status chips, and inputs. The monospaced cadence guarantees vertical numeric alignment across double-entry reconciliations without dynamic shifting.
- **Micro-Labels & Data Caps**: Section headers and table keys enforce `label-caps` in all uppercase with widened tracking (`0.1em`) to create definitive horizontal visual anchors.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout implements a high-density, mathematical column framework built for dual-ledger comparisons, variance engines, and deep audit hierarchies:

- **Desktop (1440px+)**: 12-column fixed-margin system (`margin-desktop: 3rem`, `gutter-desktop: 1.5rem`). Facilitates simultaneous parallel streams (e.g., General Ledger vs. Bank Feed) within a single zero-scroll viewport.
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px)**: 8-column layout (`margin-tablet: 2rem`, `gutter: 1rem`) with side-by-side reconciliation panels converting into synchronized tabbed workspaces.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column flow with tight outer safe zones (`margin: 1rem`). Dense financial rows reflow to priority delta cards displaying variance net values.
- **Rhythm & Padding**: All inner spacing obeys an uncompromising 4px/8px interval. Internal table row padding stays constrained (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) to maximize visible records per viewport.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids blurred atmospheric drops, heavy shadows, and artificial skeumorphism. Hierarchy is constructed solely through tonal surface staging and razor-thin boundary illumination:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#000000` true black.
- **Layer 1 (Tiled Paneling)**: `#080808` bound by a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Modules & Cards)**: `#0d0d0e` bordered with 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`.
- **Layer 3 (Overlays & Command Palettes)**: `#141416` backed by a 24px backdrop blur (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`) and framed with 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`.
- **Boundary Gradients**: Strategic high-tier cards utilize hairline gradient borders running from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` at the top edge to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` at the bottom edge, simulating an overhead directional wireframe light without shadow bleed.
- **Whisper Cyan Focus**: Focused or active items take a razor-thin 1px `#67e8f9` line paired with a subtle, non-radiant internal stroke, rejecting bloated neon blurs.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy is strictly deadpan and orthogonal (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Absolute Geometry**: Every UI element—including buttons, input wrappers, modal canvases, audit tables, filter chips, and balance indicators—is rendered with razor-sharp 90-degree corners (`0px` radius).
- **Architectural Framing**: Rectilinear geometry reinforces the mathematical precision of ledger balance sheets. The zero-radius system maximizes screen real estate, aligns tabular borders flush to parent container boundaries, and eliminates ambiguous border joins.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Pure `#ffffff` surface, pitch `#000000` text in `JetBrains Mono` medium (12px), sharp rectangular edges. Hover state shifts surface to `#e4e4e7` with an instantaneous zero-duration transition.
- **Secondary (Technical Outline)**: Background transparent, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#ffffff`. Hover updates surface to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` and border to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Primary AI Execution**: Obsidian `#080808` background, 1px solid `#67e8f9` border, `#67e8f9` text. Hover fills surface with `rgba(103, 232, 249, 0.06)`.

### Form Fields & Terminal Inputs
- **Text Inputs**: Background `#080808`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#ffffff`, placeholder text `#71717a`. Font: `JetBrains Mono` 13px. Sharp square corners. Active focus converts border to 1px `#67e8f9` with zero shadow.
- **Numeric & Currency Inputs**: Right-aligned `JetBrains Mono` numbers, displaying debit/credit indicators directly inside the right border quadrant.

### Ledger Grids & Reconciliation Tables
- **Table Headers**: Background `#080808`, bottom border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Header text: `label-caps` in `#71717a`.
- **Table Rows**: Strict alternating row states `#000000` and `#050505`. Row divider: 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`. Hover highlights full row in `#0d0d0e`.
- **Discrepancy Indicators**: Misaligned cells receive a subtle 1px dashed left border in `#f87171`, accompanied by muted red text. Matched entries display `#34d399` markers.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicator**: Rectangular box (0px radius), 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, background `#000000`, padding `2px 6px`. Text in `label-caps`.
- **AI Match Confidence**: Translucent icy background `rgba(103, 232, 249, 0.05)`, 1px border `rgba(103, 232, 249, 0.25)`, text `#67e8f9` (e.g., `[ 99.9% CONFIDENCE ]`).

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes**: Pure square box (14px x 14px), 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, background `#000000`. Checked state renders a solid white square interior fill with a centered `#000000` micro-cross or check.
- **Radio Toggles**: Sharp concentric square (not circular); outer 14px square with an inner 6px solid `#67e8f9` square when selected.

### Cards & Metric Tiles
- **Structure**: Surface `#0d0d0e`, sharp corners, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Metric Presentation**: Label placed top-left in `label-caps` (`#71717a`), metric rendered in `data-metric` (`#ffffff`), and delta variance displayed at bottom in `body-sm` (`#67e8f9` or `#f87171`).
